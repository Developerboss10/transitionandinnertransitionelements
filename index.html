Page 1 · Foundation

Core Concepts Map

Ultra-compressed — scan in 2 minutes
d-Block Element ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Last electron enters d-subshell of penultimate (n−1) shell. Groups 3–12. Config: (n−1)d¹–¹⁰ ns¹–². Exceptions like Cr (3d⁵4s¹) and Cu (3d¹⁰4s¹) due to extra stability of half-filled and fully-filled configurations.
Differentiating electron goes into (n−1)d, not outermost. This unique placement gives transition elements their characteristic properties.
🧠 Mnemonic
"शेवटचा प्रवासी एका पातळी खाली फिरून d-दरवाज्यात शिरतो"
प्रवासी = last e⁻. पातळी खाली = (n−1) shell. d-दरवाजा = d-subshell.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"3d series चा शेवटचा element?" → Zn: [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s²
🚨 Trap
4s fills before 3d, but configuration is often written with (n−1)d first.
Transition Element Definition ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Atom with incomplete d-subshell OR forms at least one stable ion with incomplete d-subshell.
"Incomplete" हा मुख्य शब्द. Ground state बघा. जर पूर्ण असेल तर ions बघा. कुठेही incomplete d नाही → NOT transition.
🧠 Mnemonic
"d मध्ये रिकामी जागा असणे अनिवार्य — अटोम असो किंवा आयन!"
Incomplete d = must. Atom किंवा ion, एकातही incomplete d असले तर transition.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"कोणता transition element नाही?" → Zn/Cd/Hg options.
⚡ Shortcut
Atom d¹⁰ + सर्व ions d¹⁰ → NOT transition.
Zn, Cd, Hg Exception ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
तिघांची d¹⁰ configuration — atom मध्ये आणि +2 ion मध्ये. → NOT transition elements.
Zn: [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s², Zn²⁺: [Ar] 3d¹⁰. Cd: 4d¹⁰ 5s². Hg: 5d¹⁰ 6s². कुठल्याही form मध्ये incomplete d नाही.
🧠 Mnemonic
"झिंदगीत CD Hit होत नाही — d पूर्ण भरलेला, रिकामी जागा शून्य!"
Zn, Cd, Hg = "Hit नाही" = transition नाही. d¹⁰ = पूर्ण → scope शून्य.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Hg transition element का नाही?" → d¹⁰ atom + ion.
🚨 Trap
d-block = Transition? NO! हा #1 trap.
Key Terms ⭐⭐⭐
  • 3d series: Sc(21) → Zn(30) — 10 elements
  • 4d series: Y(39) → Cd(48)
  • 5d series: La(57) → Hg(80)
  • 6d series: Ac(89) onwards (incomplete)
  • f-block: Lanthanides (4f) + Actinides (5f) = Inner Transition
Page 2 · Properties

General Properties

Variable OS, Size, Density, MP, IE
Variable Oxidation States ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
ns आणि (n−1)d यांच्यातले energy gap खूपच कमी → दोन्ही bonding मध्ये participate → multiple OS.
ns electrons पहिले जातात (outer), मग (n−1)d. Maximum oxidation state depends on total valence electrons but stability varies across elements. Mn shows highest +7 in 3d series. Highest OS increases till Mn then decreases.
🧠 Mnemonic
"ns आणि d यांचं अंतर इतकं कमी की दोघे बाहेर पडायला तयार — एकटा, दोन, तीन… सगळे!"
Energy gap is small → एक-दोन-तीन electrons बाहेर → +1, +2, +3… OS.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"3d मध्ये highest OS?" → Mn (+7). "Most stable?" → +2, +3.
🚨 Trap
Max OS = Most stable? NO. Higher OS = strong oxidizing agent.
Atomic & Ionic Radii ⭐⭐⭐⭐
सुरुवातीला घटतो (poor d-shielding) → मग स्थिर होतो (e⁻-e⁻ repulsion balance).
Nuclear charge वाढतो. Poor shielding of d-electrons is the main reason for contraction → outer e⁻ pull → size घटतो. Repulsion balance → size stabilizes.
🧠 Mnemonic
"d-इलेक्ट्रॉन्स चोरीने आत घुसतात — न्यूक्लियस ओढतो. मग आपसात भांडण — आकार थांबतो!"
Poor shielding = pull = shrink. Repulsion balance = flat. Graph: घट → ताप.
⚡ Shortcut
Steep fall (Sc→Mn) → Flat (Fe→Zn). "Non-uniform" = key word.
🚨 Trap
"Uniform decrease" म्हणू नका. d-block मध्ये non-uniform.
Density & Melting Point ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Density: Generally increases across series but trend is not perfectly uniform. Highest → Os (5d).
MP: Max at Group 6: Cr, Mo, W (strong metallic bonding + effective d-orbital overlap). Lowest → Zn, Cd, Hg.
High melting point due to strong metallic bonding and effective overlap of d-orbitals. d⁵s¹ configuration contributes to maximum unpaired electrons and strongest bonding. d¹⁰ = no unpaired = weakest metallic bonding = min MP.
🧠 Mnemonic
"Cr, Mo, W — त्यांच्या d⁵ मध्ये सगळे अकेले, लोखंडी बंध — उष्णता सहन! Zn मध्ये कुणीच अकेला नाही — पिघळतो!"
d⁵s¹ = 6 unpaired = max MP. d¹⁰ = 0 = min MP.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Highest MP?" → W. "Highest density?" → Os. "Lowest MP 3d?" → Zn.
⚡ Shortcut
Os = Dense. W = Hot. "ओस-डेन्स, डब्ल्यू-हॉट" — 2 sec!
Ionization Energy ⭐⭐⭐⭐
सामान्यतः वाढतो पण irregular. d⁵/d¹⁰ जवळ extra stability → IE spike.
Nuclear charge वाढते → IE वाढावी. पण half-filled (Cr: 3d⁵4s¹) आणि fully-filled (Cu: 3d¹⁰4s¹) exceptionally stable.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"IE trend?" → Generally increases, irregular due to d⁵/d¹⁰.
🚨 Trap
"Smooth increase" म्हणू नका. "Irregular" हा key.
Page 3 · Colour & Magnetism

Colour + Magnetism

d-d transition, crystal field splitting, paramagnetism, spin-only formula
Colour of Ions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Colour arises due to d–d transition caused by crystal field splitting. e⁻ jump between split d-orbitals → visible light absorb → Observed colour is complementary to absorbed light.
Free ion मध्ये 5 d-orbitals degenerate. Ligands मुळे crystal field splitting होतो. e⁻ photon absorb करून jump → उरलेला light = complementary colour.
🧠 Mnemonic
"d-दरवाज्यातून उडी मारली — एक रंग गिळला, उरलेला रंग डोळ्यांना दिसला!"
Cu²⁺ लाल गिळतो → निळा-हिरवा दिसतो. Ti³⁺ पिवळा-हिरवा गिळतो → जांभळा.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Sc³⁺ colourless पण Ti³⁺ purple का?" → Sc³⁺ = d⁰.
🚨 Trap
d⁰ (Sc³⁺) आणि d¹⁰ (Zn²⁺) ALWAYS colourless!
Paramagnetism ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Unpaired electrons → magnetic field ओढते. μ = √[n(n+2)] BM (Valid only for spin-only cases; orbital contribution neglected)
जास्त unpaired e⁻ = जास्त paramagnetism. n = ION मधील unpaired e⁻. Diamagnetic = सगळे paired.
🧠 Mnemonic
"अकेला इलेक्ट्रॉन — चुंबकाने प्रेमाने ओढला. √n(n+2) लक्षात ठेव!"
n=1→1.73, n=2→2.83, n=3→3.87, n=4→4.90, n=5→5.92 BM.
⚡ Shortcut
5 values: 1.73 | 2.83 | 3.87 | 4.90 | 5.92. Direct match.
🚨 Trap
NEUTRAL atom मध्ये unpaired शोधणं. ION ची config लिहा!

Page 4 · Applications

Catalytic + Complex Formation

Exact textbook reasons
Catalytic Properties ⭐⭐⭐⭐
तीन कारण: Variable oxidation states, Surface adsorption, Intermediate formation (lowers activation energy).
Variable oxidation states → easy e⁻ transfer. Large surface area → adsorption of reactants. Intermediate complex formation → bond breaking easier → lower activation energy.
🧠 Mnemonic
"कॅटालिस्टचं काम VIS — Variable OS, Intermediate complex, Surface adsorption!"
V = Variable OS, I = Intermediate formation, S = Surface adsorption.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Haber's?" → Fe. "Contact?" → V₂O₅. "Vanaspati?" → Ni.
⚡ Shortcut
"Catalytic reason" → "variable oxidation states" किंवा "intermediate formation".
Complex Formation ⭐⭐⭐⭐
High charge density + availability of orbitals (d, s, p) → lone pairs स्वीकारते → Lewis acid.
Small size + high charge = high charge density. Availability of orbitals (d, s, p) enables complex formation.
🧠 Mnemonic
"छोटा + जास्त charge = ताकदवान. d, s, p खोल्या रिकाम्या — ligand आला, lone pair ठेवून गेला!"
High density = strong pull. Vacant orbitals = space for lone pairs = Lewis acid.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Complex formation का?" → "High charge density + availability of orbitals (d, s, p)" option.
🚨 Trap
"Completely filled d-orbitals" → चुकीचा! High charge density + vacant orbitals हा अचूक.
Alloy Formation ⭐⭐⭐
Easily form alloys — similar atomic sizes + similar crystal structures + similar valency and metallic bonding.
d-block मध्ये radii फार बदलत नाहीत (~10-15 pm). Similar size → atoms replace each other in lattice. Similar valency and metallic bonding also contribute.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Alloys का बनतात?" → Similar atomic sizes + structures + valency.
⚡ Shortcut
"Similar atomic radii" दिसलाच → alloy answer.
Page 5 · f-Block

Inner Transition Elements

Lanthanides, Actinides, Contraction
Lanthanides & Actinides ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Lanthanides: Ce(58) → Lu(71) — 4f. "Rare Earths".
Actinides: Th(90) → Lr(103) — 5f. बहुतांश radioactive.
Lanthanides follow La and actinides follow Ac; placed separately for convenience. "Inner transition" कारण e⁻ (n-2)f मध्ये जातो.
🧠 Mnemonic
"लँथेनाइड = चौथ्या मजल्यावर f-खोली. ॲक्टिनाइड = पाचव्या मजल्यावर. दोघे आत लपलेले!"
"Inner" कारण f = (n-2). 4f = Lanthanoids, 5f = Actinoids.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"किती लँथेनाइड्स?" → 14 (Ce to Lu).
🚨 Note
Called rare earths historically, though many are relatively abundant.
Lanthanide Contraction ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Ce ते Lu पर्यंत radii steady decrease. Reason: 4f orbitals are highly diffused and show poor shielding.
14 elements भर nuclear charge +14. 4f orbitals are highly diffused and show poor shielding. Net: outer ns पूर्ण +14 effective pull → shrinkage ~10-15 pm.
🧠 Mnemonic
"4f इलेक्ट्रॉन्स स्वार्थी — न्यूक्लियसचा आकर्षण स्वतःचा घेतात, बाहेरच्यांना शिल्ड करत नाहीत!"
4f shielding खूपच कमी. प्रत्येक +1 effectively outer e⁻ pull करते.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Contraction reason?" → 4f orbitals are highly diffused and show poor shielding.
🚨 Trap
"Poor shielding by d-electrons" → चुक! Strictly 4f!
Effects of Contraction ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Effect 1: 4d & 5d same group → same size → separation कठीण (Zr-Hf, Nb-Ta, Mo-W).
Effect 2: Basic strength घटते La(OH)₃ → Lu(OH)₃.
Hf ला 14 extra protons. Contraction compensates → Zr(160) ≈ Hf(159pm). Smaller ion → M-O stronger → O-H weaker → less basic.
🧠 Mnemonic
"आकार घटला — Zr-Hf जोडीदार झाले, वेगळे करणे अशक्य! La-Lu लहान — basic ताकद कमी!"
Size match = separation nightmare. Small = strong M-O = less basic.
🎯 Exam Trigger
"Separate कठीण?" → Zr-Hf. "Basic order?" → La(OH)₃ > Lu(OH)₃.
⚡ Shortcut
4d + 5d same group pair = "difficult to separate".
